About us

Three Gingers Holdings LLC is an independent app development studio focused on creating clean, reliable, and genuinely useful applications. We take a small number of ideas and see them through with care — building for iOS and the broader Apple ecosystem.

Our work spans the full product lifecycle: concept, design, development, and ongoing support. Every app we ship is built to meet Apple's standards for quality, privacy, and user experience.
